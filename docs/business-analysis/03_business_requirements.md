# 2. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the business requirements for the ShopSphere 360 Business Intelligence and Customer Analytics Platform.

The document translates stakeholder needs and business problems into measurable requirements that will guide data modeling, analytics development, dashboard design and business recommendations.

---

# 3. Business Need

ShopSphere requires a centralized analytics solution that integrates sales, customer, product, marketing, returns and customer support information.

The solution should allow management and operational teams to monitor KPIs, investigate business performance, identify risks and opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

# 7. Requirement Acceptance Principles

A requirement will be considered satisfied when:

1. The requirement has a defined data source.
2. The required KPI or analytical output is measurable.
3. The result can be validated.
4. The result is understandable to the intended stakeholder.
5. The requirement contributes to a documented business objective.

---

# 8. Requirement Change Management

Any new requirement introduced after the initial scope shall be evaluated based on:

- Business value
- Technical feasibility
- Development effort
- Project timeline
- Impact on existing requirements
- Impact on data requirements

Requirements with significant scope impact shall be reviewed before implementation.
