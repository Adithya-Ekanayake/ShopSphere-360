# ShopSphere 360 — Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this stakeholder analysis is to identify individuals and groups affected by the ShopSphere 360 project, understand their interests and influence, identify their information requirements, and establish an appropriate engagement strategy.

# 4. Power / Interest Grid

## Manage Closely

High Power + High Interest

- CEO
- CFO
- Sales Manager
- Marketing Manager
- Business Analyst
- Data Analyst

These stakeholders should receive frequent updates and participate in major decisions.

---

## Keep Satisfied

High Power + Low/Medium Interest

- Operations Manager
- IT / Database Administrator

These stakeholders should receive important project updates and be consulted when decisions affect their responsibilities.

---

## Keep Informed

Low/Medium Power + High Interest

- Customer Experience Manager
- Sales Team
- Marketing Team
- Customer Support Team

These stakeholders should receive regular progress information and have opportunities to provide feedback.

---

## Monitor

Low Power + Low Interest

External or indirectly affected stakeholders.

These stakeholders require limited communication unless their involvement increases.

# 8. Stakeholder Engagement Principles

The project will follow the following principles:

1. Business requirements should be validated before dashboard development.
2. KPIs should have clearly defined business meanings.
3. Dashboard design should be based on stakeholder decisions rather than visual preferences.
4. Stakeholders should be involved in validating analytical findings.
5. Conflicting requirements should be prioritized according to business value.
6. Sensitive business information should be appropriately protected.
7. Recommendations should be supported by measurable evidence.

---

# 9. Key Stakeholder Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Conflicting stakeholder requirements | High | Requirement prioritization |
| Lack of stakeholder availability | High | Scheduled review sessions |
| Unclear KPI definitions | High | KPI dictionary |
| Changing business priorities | Medium | Change management process |
| Low dashboard adoption | Medium | User-centered dashboard design |
| Misinterpretation of analytical results | High | Document assumptions and methodology |

---

# 10. Stakeholder Success Criteria

Stakeholder engagement will be considered successful when:

- Key stakeholders have clearly defined requirements.
- Dashboard requirements are linked to business decisions.
- KPIs are agreed upon.
- Stakeholder priorities are documented.
- Analytical findings can be traced back to business questions.
- Stakeholders can use the final dashboards to support decisions.
